Draw Your Way

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

🔒 Your Privacy is Our Priority

Draw Your Way does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. We believe in providing a fun, creative physics puzzle experience without compromising your privacy.

What We DON'T Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we never collect:

  • No personal information – We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identifying information.
  • No location data – We never access or track your geographic location.
  • No contacts or photos – We have zero access to your contacts, photos, or files.
  • No account required – Play instantly without registration or sign-up.

Technical Data for Performance

To ensure Draw Your Way runs smoothly at high frame rates and to optimize 3D physics rendering on your device, minimal anonymous technical data may be processed:

  • Device Type & OS Version: To optimize 3D graphics and shader compatibility for your specific device.
  • Crash Reports: If the app encounters an unexpected exception, anonymous logs help us resolve the issue swiftly.
  • Performance Metrics: Frame rendering speed and physics calculation timing to guarantee smooth 60+ FPS gameplay.

⚡ This technical data is completely anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Third-Party Data Sharing

🚫 We Never Share Your Data

Under no circumstances do we sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties. Your gaming experience stays strictly between you and Draw Your Way.

We do not integrate third-party ad tracking networks or analytics trackers that profile user behaviors across apps.

Data Storage

All your game progress, level unlock status, star ratings, and custom drawing scores are stored locally on your device. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • You maintain complete control over your saved progress
  • Uninstalling the app cleanly removes all associated local game files

Children's Privacy

Draw Your Way is designed to be family-friendly and safe for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no risk of children's information being collected or misused. Parents can feel confident letting children enjoy drawing creative 3D bridges and puzzles.

Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect any updates to the game. Changes will be posted here with an updated revision date.
